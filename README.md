# FlyBy ✈️

**Never let an email die in someone else's inbox again.**

The biggest problem in email isn't spam — it's people. You send a request and tick it
off your list; the other person sits on it; the deadline burns. FlyBy keeps watch
for you: tell it *"bring this back in 3 days if nobody answers"* and forget about it.
If a reply arrives, FlyBy quietly stands down. Set it with one tap from the FlyBy panel, or from any phone by [BCC-ing yourself a code like `+3d`](KB.md#3-setting-a-reminder). If it doesn't, the thread lands
back on top of your inbox — or FlyBy sends the [polite nudge you wrote in advance](KB.md#4-what-happens) for you.

It's a home-grown, zero-cost follow-up tool living
right inside Gmail (web and mobile) and running on your own Google account — no
subscription, no third party reading your mail.

Also: a Chrome-extension "Remind me" button right next to Send, plus a coloured band on every FlyBy thread that tells you at a glance whether it is waiting, answered or came back ([KB §7](KB.md#7-chrome-extension)),
private notes on any conversation, and recurring emails.

## Install

FlyBy runs as your own copy under your own Google account, so installing means making that copy: about 15 minutes, the
same on Mac and Windows, nothing to install but Chrome. Download three files from the
[latest release](https://github.com/Marcus1571/flyby/releases/latest), paste two of them into a new Apps Script project,
click through Google's consent screen, and load the extension into Chrome. Every click is in the
[step-by-step install guide](KB.md#2-install-once-per-google-account).
