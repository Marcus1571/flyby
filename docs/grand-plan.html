Project FlyBy · Grand plan · November 2026

Every email gets an answer — or comes back to you

One idea, two engines. Gmail users get reminders right inside Gmail, running free on Google. Yahoo and AOL users get the same promise from an engine on a VPS. Orange = already built.

Who uses what

The road, phase by phase

Each phase works on its own. P2 and P3 can swap places if Alex and Sam matter more than the extras.

What each person actually does

Gmail · laptop + phone

You

  • Laptop (from P1): right next to Send, tick “Remind me · in 2 days · if no reply” next to Send.
  • Today, laptop or phone: open the sent email, tap a preset in the FlyBy panel.
  • Shortcut anywhere: add me+3d in Bcc while writing — no clicks after Send.
  • Runs on Google for free; a VPS only needed for the P2 extras.

Apple Mail on a MacBook · Yahoo

Alex

  • Today, free — but only half of it: Apple Mail’s “Remind Me” brings an email back at a time they pick, even if they already replied; its “Follow Up” suggestions check for no reply, but Apple decides which emails and when.
  • With FlyBy (P3): nothing to learn. FlyBy watches every email they send; if nobody answers in 3 working days they get a reminder email with buttons: “Remind me again in 2 days” · “Stop watching”.
  • Works the same in Apple Mail on the Mac, on their iPhone and on Yahoo’s website.
  • Maybe (P4): a FlyBy button in Apple Mail’s compose window, to pick a different deadline.

AOL Mail

Sam

  • With FlyBy (P3): same as Alex — nothing to learn, works whatever program they use on Windows.
  • One setup step: turn on AOL 2-step verification and create an app password for FlyBy.
  • Or: move to Gmail and get the full experience.
